# Agney Praseed — Personal Website

> This is the personal website of Agney Praseed, a software engineer turned AI researcher based in Berlin, Germany. The site showcases his professional background, projects, skills, and personal interests.

## About

Agney Praseed (he/him) is currently pursuing a Master's in Artificial Intelligence. He previously worked for nearly two years as a Software Development Engineer building production-grade backend systems with Java, Spring Boot, and PostgreSQL, and shipping full-stack features across cloud platforms with AWS and Azure.

His current research interests lie in mechanistic interpretability, multimodality, and diffusion models.

## Projects

- **Nano Curator**: An AI-powered personal stylist. Upload your photo and get curated outfit makeovers, haircut suggestions, and a sustainable fashion report. Built with React, TypeScript, and Gemini. [GitHub](https://github.com/Agneypraseed/Nano-Curator)
- **OpenMatch**: An explainable resume-to-role matching workspace for applicants and recruiters. Every score links back to evidence, with skill-gap analysis, resume guidance, and interview preparation. Built with React, FastAPI, Python, LangChain, and FAISS. [GitHub](https://github.com/Agneypraseed/OpenMatch)
- **Neural Canvas**: Optimization-based neural style transfer that preserves content structure while borrowing texture from a style reference. A frozen VGG-19 guides pixel optimization through content, style, and total-variation losses. Built with Python, PyTorch, and Gradio. [GitHub](https://github.com/Agneypraseed/neural-canvas)
